Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Vista Coffee, part of Vista Group, handles the information you provide through this website.

Information we collect

When you send an inquiry or subscribe to our newsletter, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, company, country, email address, phone or WhatsApp number and the content of your message.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry, quotation or sample request.
  • To send you news and updates, if you have subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Third-party services

Form submissions are delivered to our sales team by email through a form-processing service. This website also uses Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map, which may collect technical data such as your IP address.

Your choices

To ask about, correct or delete the information you have shared with us, email info@vistacoffeerw.com.
