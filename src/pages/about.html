Our Story

A Rwandan Coffee Company with a Global Outlook

Vista Coffee is a Rwandan coffee company and part of Vista Group. Based in Kigali, we focus on coffee sourcing, processing, roasting, branding and international trade.

Our business is built around Rwanda's rich coffee-growing heritage, strong agricultural communities and growing global demand for specialty coffee. We create sustainable commercial opportunities by connecting farmers and cooperatives with domestic and international markets.

Vista Group is an international group active in agriculture, food and beverage, machinery, transportation and logistics.

{{icon:eye}} Our Vision

To become a recognized international coffee company, bringing the exceptional quality and unique identity of Rwandan coffee to markets around the world.

{{icon:target}} Our Mission

To source, process, roast and supply premium Rwandan coffee while building long-term partnerships, supporting agricultural communities and delivering consistent quality to our customers.
