Origin & Varieties

We work through relationships with coffee producers, washing stations and cooperatives across Rwanda's coffee-growing regions.

Our focus is Rwandan Arabica, including the Bourbon variety for which Rwanda's highlands are known.

  • Relationships with producers, washing stations and cooperatives
  • Arabica Bourbon
  • Grown at altitude in volcanic soils
{{img:drying-beds|Coffee drying on raised beds under a shade structure}}

Processing

Processing shapes the character of every cup. We offer coffee from the following methods, subject to availability:

  • Fully washed — clean, bright and clear in the cup
  • Natural — dried in the cherry for fruit-forward sweetness
  • Honey — part of the fruit left on during drying for added body
