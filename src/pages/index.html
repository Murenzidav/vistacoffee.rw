Welcome to Vista Coffee

Discover the Excellence of Rwandan Coffee

At Vista Coffee, we bring the rich heritage and exceptional quality of Rwandan coffee to customers around the world. From the fertile highlands of Rwanda to the global marketplace, we are committed to delivering premium coffee through responsible sourcing, careful processing and consistent quality.

As part of Vista Group, we combine local expertise with an international business network to connect Rwandan coffee producers with roasters, distributors, retailers and coffee enthusiasts worldwide.
